# Sake

Current art:

- `sake.png` - held gourd and thrown gourd
- `sake-droplet.png` - sake spit/spill/vomit effect particles
- `drunken-swirls.png` - visual effect during the 15 second drunk state

Moves:

- LMB, Drink: 5 second cooldown. One equal 1/3 outcome: +25% melee damage for 5s, 25% damage reduction for 5s, or 8 health regenerated over 5s. Drinking stores a mouthful for Q.
- Q, Sake Spit: available after drinking. Fires a green outlined cone, damages and knocks back, 4 second cooldown.
- Q, Sake Spill: used when no mouthful is stored. Tips the gourd and leaves a #e2a417 puddle that damages and knocks an enemy into a short slip ragdoll, 5 second cooldown.
- E, Chug: 25 second cooldown. For 15 seconds, damage, defense, movement, and jump strength are increased by 50%. Drunken swirls replace the normal buff particles. When it ends, the fighter vomits a stronger green outlined puddle.
- Drunk LMB, Gourd Throw: 3 second cooldown. Throws toward the cursor. Fighter hits cause damage plus 3 seconds of bleed. Surface hits create fading #bf7950 shard rectangles. Shards stop dealing damage once their fade reaches 50%.
