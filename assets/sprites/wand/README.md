# Wand

Current art:

- `wand.png` - held weapon and loadout icon
- `star-small.png` - primary, burst fragments, and homing stars
- `star-large.png` - Q bouncing star

Current moves:

- LMB: larger small spinning star, low damage, passes through map solids.
- Q: requires full charge. Large star, low contact damage, passes through fighters, ricochets once using its impact angle, then bursts into twelve small radial stars on the next terrain impact.
- E: three larger low-damage small stars in a spread, slight homing toward the nearest fighter, fade over about three seconds, disappear on fighter contact.
